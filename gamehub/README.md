# Game Hub (offline)
Download `index.html` and double-click it — runs entirely on your PC, no internet or install needed.
Games: Snake, Breakout, 2048, Tic-Tac-Toe, Memory, Flappy. Best scores save locally in your browser.

## ✋ Hand control (webcam)
Click **Hand control** in the top bar and allow the camera. Open in Chrome or Edge.
- Your index fingertip moves a yellow cursor.
- **Pinch** your thumb and index finger together to click (pick tiles, choose games, flap in Flappy).
- Move your hand toward the **edge** of the camera view (left/right/up/down) to send arrow keys (Snake, 2048, Breakout).

The first time you turn it on, it needs internet to download the hand-tracking model. Video stays on your PC.
