Common Questions

You Might Be Wondering…

Quick, honest answers about getting started, pricing, and support.

What happens when we first sign up?
We start with a friendly conversation and a quick look at your computers, network, and email. Then we set up our tools, write down how everything works, and start fixing the most annoying problems first — most clients feel the difference within the first couple of weeks.
How much does it cost?
Most clients pay a simple, predictable monthly amount based on how many people or computers they have. That covers the watching, protecting, and helping. Bigger one-time projects (like moving offices) get their own clear quote first — no surprise bills, ever.
Our team works from home sometimes. Is that okay?
Absolutely. We make sure everyone can work safely from home, the office, or on the road — and we're just as easy to reach no matter where your team is that day.
How do you keep us safe from scams and hackers?
We add an extra lock to your accounts (so a stolen password isn't enough), filter out dangerous emails before they reach you, keep your computers updated, and back everything up nightly. If something ever does go wrong, we have a plan ready and tested.
We already have an IT person. Can you still help?
Of course. We're happy to be the full IT department or just pitch in — handling the day-to-day calls, the updates, or a big project — while your person focuses on what they do best.
How fast can we start?
We can usually chat this week and start right away. Getting fully set up typically takes two to four weeks, but you'll feel the difference much sooner.
