About

A simpler way through the eviction process.

Evict Point works with small and mid-size landlords across New York State.

Most landlords don’t need a law firm to serve a rent demand or file a petition — they need someone who knows the paperwork, keeps track of the deadlines and answers the phone. That’s what we do.

We handle the notices, filings, service and coordination involved in landlord–tenant cases, and keep you updated along the way, so you can get back to running your property.

How we work

Clear pricing before we start. Documents prepared carefully and on time. One point of contact who knows your case.

Evict Point is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. If you need advice about your situation, please consult a licensed New York attorney.

Ready to get started?

The request form takes about ten minutes. If you have the lease and a rent ledger, keep them handy.
