## 1. What LinearWatch is

LinearWatch is a **disclosed** employee-monitoring service. It periodically captures
screenshots from Customer-designated, **company-owned** devices and makes them
available to authorized administrators in a dashboard. LinearWatch is designed to be
visible to monitored users: the desktop agent shows an always-visible system-tray
indicator whenever it is running and presents a consent notice on first run.

## 2. Acceptable use — disclosed monitoring only

You may use LinearWatch **only** where all of the following are true:

1. **Company-owned devices only.** The monitored device is owned or controlled by
   Customer and issued for work purposes. You will not deploy the agent on personal
   or BYOD devices without a separate, documented legal basis and the device owner's
   consent.
2. **Notice and consent.** Before monitoring begins, you will **notify each monitored
   user** that the device is monitored, what is captured (screen contents), how often,
   how long it is retained, and who can view it — and you will **obtain any consent or
   works-council/employee-representative approval required by the laws that apply to
   that user** (which may include jurisdictions where the user works or resides).
3. **Visible indicator preserved.** You will not attempt to hide, disable, spoof, or
   circumvent the tray indicator, the consent notice, or any other transparency
   feature.
4. **Lawful purpose.** You will use captured data only for legitimate, disclosed
   business purposes and in compliance with all applicable laws (including labor,
   privacy, wiretap/interception, and data-protection laws).

## 3. Prohibited use

You will **not**, and will not permit anyone to:

- Deploy or use LinearWatch **covertly**, or on any person who has not been notified
  as required by law;
- Monitor personal devices, or capture activity outside the scope disclosed to the
  monitored user;
- Use the service to capture special-category / sensitive data where prohibited, or to
  target individuals based on protected characteristics, union activity, or lawful
  off-duty conduct;
- Modify, repackage, or distribute the agent to remove or obscure the monitoring
  indicator or consent step;
- Use the service to violate any person's privacy, employment, or human rights.

**Covert or non-consensual use is a material breach of these Terms and is grounds for
immediate suspension or termination** of your account, without refund, in addition to
any other remedies.

## 4. Customer responsibilities as data controller

As between the parties, Customer is the **data controller** (or equivalent) for data

- Establishing and documenting a lawful basis for monitoring;
