Set Up Printing & Scanning

Windows already knows how to talk to the Brother copier. You just add the printer, then install the free Windows Scan app to scan documents back to your computer.

Get the Windows Scan App

Step 1 — Add the printer

  1. Make sure your computer is on the same Wi-Fi / network as the copier, and the copier is turned on.
  2. Click the Windows Start button and open Settings.
  3. Go to Bluetooth & devices, then Printers & scanners.
  4. Click Add device and wait a few seconds while it searches.
  5. When Brother DCP-L2640DW appears in the list, click Add device next to it.
  6. Windows installs everything on its own. When it stops saying “Setting up,” you can print.

Step 2 — Install the scan app

  1. Click the green Get the Windows Scan App button above.
  2. The Microsoft Store opens. Click Get (or Install) — it is free.
  3. Wait for it to finish. You only do this once.

Step 3 — How to scan

  1. Put the pages face-down on the glass, or face-up in the top feeder.
  2. Click Start, type Scan, and open the Scan app.
  3. Check that Brother DCP-L2640DW is shown at the top. If not, pick it from the dropdown.
  4. Under File type, choose PDF for documents or JPEG for photos.
  5. Click Scan. The pages go through and the file is saved to your Pictures > Scans folder.
  6. Click View to open it, or drag it wherever you need it.

Windows 10 & 11 · Printer must be on and on the same network · No driver download needed
