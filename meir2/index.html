Common questions

General information only, not legal advice.

What is a 14-day rent demand?

Before starting a nonpayment case in New York, a landlord generally has to serve the tenant with a written demand for the rent owed, giving them 14 days to pay.

What's the difference between nonpayment and holdover?

A nonpayment case asks for unpaid rent and possession. A holdover case asks for possession when a tenant stays after the lease or tenancy has ended.

How much notice is needed to end a tenancy?

It depends on how long the tenant has lived there: 30 days for under a year, 60 days for one to two years, and 90 days for two years or more.

How long does an eviction take?

It varies by court and county, and by whether the tenant contests the case. We'll give you a realistic estimate once we've reviewed your request.

What documents should I have ready?

The lease or rental agreement, a rent ledger or payment history, and copies of any notices you've already served. You can upload them on the form.

Ready to get started?

The request form takes about ten minutes. If you have the lease and a rent ledger, keep them handy.
