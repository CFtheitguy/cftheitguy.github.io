Reading your file…
Your emails stay private. This page opens the file directly inside your own browser. Nothing is sent to us or to any website — you can even turn off your internet and it still works.
  • 1. Find the file It is usually in your Downloads folder, from Google Takeout or an archive program. A .zip is fine — no need to unzip it.
  • 2. Open it here Choose it above and wait a moment while it loads.
  • 3. Read & search Click any email to read it. Use the search box to find one, and Print to save it as a PDF.
