Steering

On a phone or iPad, touch and hold anywhere — a stick appears where your thumb lands, and sliding left or right banks the aircraft. On a computer use ← → or A D. Throttle is always wide open; the only thing you control is the line.

The pylons

Every pylon has to be rounded on the outside — clip inside one and it's a two second penalty on your race time. Hard banking scrubs speed, so the quick way round is the smooth line, not the tightest one.

Nobody crashes

There are no wrecks in this game — a bad line costs you seconds, never an aircraft. Ranks, best laps and unlocks are saved in this browser only. No account, no server, nothing to download.
