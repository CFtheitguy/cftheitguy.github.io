Eviction Help for Ulster County Landlords

Evict Point works with landlords and property managers throughout Ulster County — from New Paltz and Saugerties to Ellenville and the county seat of Kingston.

We handle the paperwork, deadlines and coordination involved in a landlord–tenant case so you can focus on your property. Whether it’s a nonpayment case or a holdover, we help you get it started correctly and keep it moving.

Where Ulster County cases are heard

Eviction cases in Ulster County are generally heard in Kingston City Court, and the Town and Village Justice Courts elsewhere in the county. We help prepare the right paperwork for the court that handles your property and keep track of your court dates.

How We Help Ulster County Landlords

Eviction Notices

14-day rent demands, 30/60/90-day notices of termination and notices to cure, prepared and served.

Petitions & Filings

Nonpayment and holdover petitions prepared and filed with the local court.

Process Serving

Personal and substituted service, with notarized affidavits of service.

Court Support

Scheduling and coordination for your court dates.

Warrants

Warrant requests and coordination with the marshal or sheriff.
