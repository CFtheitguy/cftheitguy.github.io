About Evict Point

Professional. Local. Trusted.

Focused on What Matters

Our mission is simple: to provide landlords with reliable, efficient and affordable eviction services so you can protect your property and move forward.

Evict Point works with small and mid-size landlords and property owners across New York State. We take care of the paperwork, deadlines and coordination involved in the landlord–tenant process, and keep you informed at every step.

Why Landlords Choose Us

  • Experienced & knowledgeable
  • Fast turnaround times
  • Accurate & court-ready documents
  • Clear communication
  • Professional & reliable
  • Affordable — a cost-effective alternative to most attorneys

Evict Point is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. If you need legal advice about your situation, please consult a licensed New York attorney.
