Our Experience

Evict Point specializes in providing administrative and eviction-support services to landlords and property owners throughout New York.

We assist with document preparation, service coordination, filing coordination, scheduling, and other case-related administrative tasks. Our team is experienced in working with landlords and property managers to help keep the eviction process organized from start to finish.

When legal advice or legal representation is needed, we work with or refer matters to a licensed New York attorney.
